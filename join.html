Join us


Criterion Arbitrage & Trading is continuously on the look-out for highly motivated trading talent. You have a degree, are at the start of your career. Figures speak louder than words to you.

You are best of your class, school, year, in what you do, and ready to be tested. We have room for you, sharing our skills, passion, tension and rewards.

Who we're looking for

Our trading positions suit candidates who combine an analytical mind with the drive to compete in fast-moving markets. If the following sounds like you, we would like to hear from you:

  • A completed academic degree in a quantitative field, such as econometrics, mathematics, physics, business or finance
  • A sharp, analytical mind with a genuine affinity for numbers, probability and financial markets
  • A competitive, results-driven attitude and the resilience to perform under pressure
  • The ability to make fast, well-founded decisions with incomplete information
  • A strong team player who also takes ownership of their own results
  • An entrepreneurial mindset and a genuine hunger to keep learning and improving
