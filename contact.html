Why work at Criterion

Beyond the role itself, joining Criterion means joining a small, ambitious team where your contribution is felt directly. A few of the advantages our people mention most:

  • A competitive salary with a bonus structure directly tied to trading performance
  • Hands-on responsibility from day one, with real capital and real decisions
  • A flat, collegial team where partners trade alongside you on the desk
  • Ongoing coaching and training to sharpen your trading skills
  • A prime location at Beursplein 5, in the heart of Amsterdam's financial district
  • Modern trading technology and infrastructure to support fast decision-making
