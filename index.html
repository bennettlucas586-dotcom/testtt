Welcome to Criterion Arbitrage & Trading

Criterion Arbitrage & Trading BV is an independent proprietary trading firm based at Beursplein 5 in the heart of Amsterdam. Since 2011 we have been active as a market maker and arbitrageur in listed derivatives, providing liquidity on the major European cash and derivatives exchanges.

Built on the experience of our partners, Criterion combines disciplined risk management with fast, technology-driven decision making. We are a small, close-knit team with a big ambition: to be a serious and respected player in the proprietary wholesale trading segment.
